# Worklog — PERF-15: tunable cache caps, perf-neutral

## What was done

`cacheLimitMb()` in `rendering.ts` (exported, reused by `app.ts`
gzip cache): per-cache `CAMPFIRE_<FRAGMENT|PAGE|GZIP|BODY>_CACHE_MB`
with `CAMPFIRE_CACHE_MB` fallback, 32MB defaults. Unset env =
byte-identical behavior (verified: defaults bench matches the band).

Validation, 4 workers, mixed read+post blast (fills all stores):

| caps | room | sidebar | search | post | room2 | peak MB |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| default 32M | 25,133 | 26,886 | 19,671 | 3,845 | 24,017 | 1,056 |
| 4M | 25,177 | 27,655 | 19,446 | 3,936 | 24,239 | 785 |

Zero perf loss, -26% peak (-22% current). Remaining ~670MB is
per-process runtime baseline x6 processes + allocator retention
after the blast, not the bounded stores.

## Key decisions and why

- Honest caveat (in AGENT.md): 4MB holds ~8 room pages. Neutral
  on concentrated workloads (bench), but large working sets evict
  more. Defaults stay 32MB = the benchmarked performance; small
  servers opt in knowingly. No universal free lunch claimed.
