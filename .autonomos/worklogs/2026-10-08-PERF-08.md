# Worklog — PERF-08: cable scaling at 500/1000 clients

## What was done

Benched cable at 500 and 1000 clients (4 workers, tput 15s,
posters 4), Bun vs Rust on the same host:

| clients | Bun msg/s (fr/s), p50/p99 | Rust msg/s (fr/s), p50/p99 |
|---|---|---|
| 100 | 814 (81k), 3.0/52ms | 1472 (147k), 1.5/7.3ms |
| 500 | 342 (171k), 6.2/14.8ms | 407 (204k), 5.4/12.6ms |
| 1000 | 156 (156k), 11.1/16.1ms | 201 (201k), 9.4/17.6ms |

No collapse: all clients ready, connect <0.2s, frames/s scales to
~200k for both. At 500-1000 the gap is x1.2-1.3 on throughput with
equal latency; the 100-client gap is the POST writer lock (each
message costs one HTTP POST).

Correctness fix found en route: `bodyHtmlCache` had no eviction —
28k bench POSTs grew it past 280MB (container at 390MB). Bounded it
at 32MB LRU like the fragment store (+LRU touch on hit). 53 tests
pass, typecheck clean.

## Key decisions and why

- No cable code change needed at scale; the remaining gap follows
  POST throughput (PERF-06/PERF-10 territory).
