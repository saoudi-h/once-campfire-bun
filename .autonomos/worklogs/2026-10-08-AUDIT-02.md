  reads `reference/` directly — switching pins would change assets
  and require re-validation. No audit requirement forces the Rust pin.
- No remote configured, so virgin-clone proof used a `file://` clone
  of a temp commit (reset after). True remote-clone check remains for
  whoever sets the remote.
